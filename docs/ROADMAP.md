Next decision comes from the M1 Mac build/live test. The product plan requires a stop
at hardware gates. Independent source inventory is permissible; dependent audio
features do not advance until the user supplies a PASS or explicitly defers the gate.

Research must preserve statuses per claim: VERIFIED, CORROBORATED, UNVERIFIED — WEB
RESEARCH, UNKNOWN. Audio-match support cannot promote historical verification.
Factory and calibration data remain separate. Match score and confidence are separate.
